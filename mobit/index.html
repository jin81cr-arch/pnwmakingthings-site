MOBIT privacy policy

MOBIT: Habit & Money Tracker. Made by PNW Making Things.
Effective November 12, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

Privacy Policy

MOBIT is a habit and money tracker made by PNW Making Things ("we"). Everything you put into MOBIT stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, receive or store any of it. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some device information as explained under Ads.

What stays on your phone

Your habits, streaks, rewards, quotes settings, money entries, investments, savings, bills, goals, photos of receipts, categories, colors and other settings are saved only on your device. If you delete the app or use Settings → Backup & Data → Erase All Data, they are gone. None of this is ever shared with Google or anyone else.

No accounts, no tracking

  • MOBIT has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • MOBIT has no analytics tools, and it doesn't track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information, because we never have it.
  • In the free version, Google collects an app-specific device identifier to show ads, as explained under Ads.

Ads (free version only)

The free version of MOBIT shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. MOBIT Pro subscribers never see ads, and for them the ad service isn't loaded at all.

  • To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as an app-specific device identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address), ad interactions and diagnostics.
  • MOBIT doesn't ask for permission to track you, so Google can't use your iPhone's advertising identifier.
  • In regions that require it, such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, MOBIT asks for your consent before any ads load. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → MOBIT Pro → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Camera and photos

If you allow it, MOBIT uses the camera or your photo library to scan receipts and screenshots. The amount is read on your phone using Apple's built-in text recognition. Photos are never uploaded.

Calendar and reminders

If you allow it, MOBIT shows your phone's calendar and can add, move or delete events and reminders you create or edit in MOBIT. To-dos you add are saved in your iPhone's Reminders app. This happens only on your phone.

Face ID and PIN

The optional app lock uses Face ID or Touch ID through iOS. MOBIT never sees your face or fingerprint data. Your PIN is stored as a scrambled code in the iPhone Keychain.

Backups

Back Up Now saves one file to the place you choose, such as iCloud Drive or Files. That file is yours. We never receive it.

Purchases

MOBIT Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles the payment and we never see your card details. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple Account has an active Pro subscription.

Children

MOBIT isn't directed at children. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Terms of Use

MOBIT Pro

  • MOBIT Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, offered monthly or yearly. The price and length are shown in the app before you buy, and payment is charged to your Apple Account.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings.
  • Use Restore Purchase in Settings to unlock Pro on a new iPhone with the same Apple Account.

Not financial advice

MOBIT helps you record and see your own numbers. It doesn't give financial, tax or investment advice.

How do I move MOBIT to a new phone?
Open Settings → Backup & Data → Back Up Now and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install MOBIT and tap Restore from Backup.
I bought Pro but it's locked.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
How do I turn off ads?
Subscribe to MOBIT Pro (monthly or yearly) in Settings. Ads go away right after you subscribe.
Face ID doesn't open the app.
Check iPhone Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Other Apps and make sure MOBIT is turned on. You can always unlock with your PIN.
How do I learn the app?
Open Settings → How to Use MOBIT for a short guide.

Need help with MOBIT?

Email support@pnwmakingthings.com and tell us what you were trying to do.
