MOBIT Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Short version: MOBIT has no account and does not connect to your bank. What you enter stays on your iPhone, and we do not collect it.

MOBIT is made by PNW Making Things ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what we do with it.

Information the app stores

The app saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • Habits, goals, rewards and your progress
  • Income, expenses, bills and earning goals you type in
  • Investment entries you type in (MOBIT never links to a bank or brokerage)
  • Screenshots or images you choose to attach
  • App settings such as colors and icons

This information is not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No bank, card or brokerage logins
  • No location
  • No contacts
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking

Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it. You can change any of these in your device settings.

  • Photos: only the images you pick, which stay on your device
  • Notifications: for habit and bill reminders you turn on

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card number or billing details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase or subscription is active. You manage or cancel subscriptions in your store account settings.

Advertising

The current version of MOBIT shows no ads. If ads are added in a later version, this policy will be updated first to explain what the ad provider collects and the choices you have.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because we hold none of it, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

MOBIT is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches you.
