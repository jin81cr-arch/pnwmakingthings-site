DigiAid privacy policy

DigiAid: Senior Digital Helper. Made by PNW Making Things.
Updated November 15, 2026.

Emergencies

DigiAid is not a replacement for 911, a medical alert device, or emergency services. Calls depend on your phone, carrier, network and settings, and we can't guarantee any call will connect. In an emergency, dial 911 directly.

Privacy policy

What we collect

DigiAid itself collects nothing. It has no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no ads and no tracking. Apple handles purchases, speech recognition, translation and message delivery under Apple's own privacy policy.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Text that DigiAid reads out loud, and photos, screenshots or pages you scan in the Camera tab, are handled on your iPhone. They are not sent to us and DigiAid does not keep the pictures.
  • The contacts you add in DigiAid (names, phone numbers, emails) and your settings are saved only on your iPhone.
  • If you open a web page in Read, your iPhone connects to that website to load the page, the same as a web browser would.
  • PDFs and documents you open in Read are read on your iPhone. They are not sent to us.

Reminders

Pill reminders, appointments, pill counts for the refill reminder, and diet notes are saved only on your iPhone. Reminders are a convenience, not medical advice, and may not appear if notifications are turned off, the phone is off, or the phone is in a Focus mode. Do not rely on DigiAid alone for taking medicine. Follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

Daily Check-In, Important Numbers and pictures

Daily Check-In settings, Important Numbers, and pictures you add to Speed Dial buttons are saved only on your iPhone. When you tap I'm OK Today, DigiAid can open a text message to the people you chose; it is sent only if you press Send. DigiAid cannot tell anyone by itself if you don't check in. It is not an emergency or monitoring service.

My Info (health information)

My Info can hold allergies, medicines, diet needs, your doctor's details and an emergency contact. This is health information. It stays only on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, we cannot see it, and it is not shared with anyone unless you choose to show or send it. If your phone is lost, unlocked, or shared with someone else, anyone holding it could see this information.

Washington residents and health information

My Info is health information you choose to enter. Because it is saved only on your iPhone and is never sent to us, PNW Making Things does not receive, collect, share or sell it. You can delete it at any time by clearing My Info or deleting the app.

Translate

Translate, and translation in Talk, use Apple's translator that is built into your iPhone. The words you type, say or point the camera at are translated by Apple's software under Apple's privacy policy. The first time you use a language, your iPhone may download it from Apple. We do not receive or keep what you translate. Translations can have mistakes. Do not use them for medicine or doctor's instructions, and check a translated message before you send it.

Contacts, voice and location

  • Contacts: if you pick someone from your iPhone Contacts, DigiAid reads only the person you choose.
  • Voice: when you speak a message, a reminder or something to translate, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or keep recordings.
  • Location: your location is used only when you tap I Need Help, Where Am I? or Save Where I Parked. For I Need Help, it goes only to the people you send it to. A saved parking spot stays on your iPhone until you clear it. DigiAid does not track your location in the background.

Setting up DigiAid for someone else

A family member can prepare a setup (contacts, reminders, My Info and settings) and send it as a file by text message, email or AirDrop. The file goes directly from one phone to the other through the method you choose. DigiAid does not store, receive or see it along the way. The person who sent the file has no access to the other phone afterward. The file is not locked with a password, so send it only to the person it is meant for.

Agreement record

Nothing is added until the person receiving the setup reviews the list and taps Add These. DigiAid saves the date and time of that choice on that iPhone only. It is not sent to us or to the sender.

Backups and Download My Data

You can save a backup file or an easy-to-read copy of your DigiAid information at any time. These files are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to save or send them. DigiAid does not keep backups anywhere else. The files are not locked with a password, so keep them somewhere safe.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple may include DigiAid's information in that backup under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it.

Permissions DigiAid may ask for

  • Camera: to magnify things, read printed text out loud, and translate signs or menus.
  • Photos: to read a picture or screenshot you choose, or to add a picture to a Speed Dial button.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: so you can speak a text, an email, a reminder or something to translate instead of typing.
  • Contacts: only if you choose to add someone from your iPhone Contacts.
  • Location: only when you tap I Need Help, Where Am I? or Save Where I Parked.
  • Notifications: to remind you about pills and appointments.

You can turn any of these off in the iPhone Settings app.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time $7.99 unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

DigiAid does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page.

Support

Need help with DigiAid? Email us: support@pnwmakingthings.com

Please tell us your iPhone model and what you were trying to do. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

  • Restore my purchase: open DigiAid > Settings (top right) > Restore Purchase.
  • Reminders are not showing: open the iPhone Settings app > Notifications > DigiAid, and turn on Allow Notifications.
  • Save a backup: open DigiAid > Settings > Backup & My Data > Save a Backup, then tap Save to Files.
  • Better voices: iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices (called Spoken Content on older iPhones), then download an Enhanced voice.
