BudgetGenie privacy policy and terms

BudgetGenie: Smart Budget. Made by PNW Making Things LLC.
Effective October 31, 2026. Updated November 26, 2026.

Privacy Policy

BudgetGenie is a budgeting app made by PNW Making Things LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores and what it shares.

The short version: your budget stays on your iPhone. BudgetGenie has no accounts on our servers, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We never see or sell your financial information.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is saved on your device only:

  • Your name, and your email if you add one
  • Your PIN, saved as a one-way scrambled code that can't be turned back into the PIN
  • Budgets, income, spending, bills, reminders, goals, debts, shopping lists, saved prices and investment balances you enter
  • Receipt photos, the text read from them, and e-receipts you paste in
  • Your app settings, including your chosen language

What we collect

Nothing. BudgetGenie doesn't send your personal or financial information to us or to anyone else. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and no advertising or device identifiers are used. We never sell data.

Receipts and the camera

When you scan a receipt or read a screenshot, your iPhone's built-in scanner and text reader run on your phone to find the edges and read the text. Receipt photos and their text are saved only on your phone and are never uploaded.

Face ID and Touch ID

Your iPhone checks your face or fingerprint. BudgetGenie never sees or stores that data. The app only gets a yes or no answer.

BudgetGenie Pro purchases

If you subscribe to BudgetGenie Pro, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Apple, on your phone, whether your subscription is active.

Family sharing (iCloud)

If you turn on Family sharing, your budget is stored in your own iCloud account and shared only with the people you invite. BudgetGenie has no server and can't see this data. You can stop sharing, or leave a shared budget, at any time in Settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Internet use

BudgetGenie works offline. It only goes online when you buy, restore or manage BudgetGenie Pro (handled by Apple) or use Family sharing (your own iCloud account).

Backups

To protect your financial information, BudgetGenie data isn't included in your iPhone's iCloud backup. If you lose your phone or delete the app, your BudgetGenie data is gone, unless you use Family sharing, in which case the shared budget stays in iCloud.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently erases everything BudgetGenie saved on your phone, including receipt photos. Deleting the app does the same. If you use Family sharing, stop sharing in Settings first. Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

BudgetGenie isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Terms of Use

Your use of BudgetGenie is covered by the PNW Making Things LLC Terms of Use and Apple's Standard License Agreement (EULA). These points also apply:

BudgetGenie Pro

BudgetGenie is free to use. BudgetGenie Pro is an optional subscription, billed monthly or yearly to your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Not financial, tax or legal advice

BudgetGenie gives estimates to help you plan. It isn't financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making big money decisions.

Estimates can be wrong

Sales tax rates, shopping totals, loan payments, goal plans, receipt readings, item categories and debt payoff dates are estimates. Always check the actual price at the register and the actual terms with your lender.

Your PIN

You're responsible for keeping your phone and PIN safe. We can't recover a forgotten PIN or lost data, because we never receive it.

No warranty

The app is provided "as is." To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses such as late fees, missed payments or money decisions made using the app.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

With Pro, turn on Family sharing in Settings and invite your family through iCloud.
