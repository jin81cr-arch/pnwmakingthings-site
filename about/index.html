About Us

Simple apps for everyday problems. Made in the Pacific Northwest.

PNW Making Things LLC is a team of two making independent apps in Washington State. Every app starts with a real problem we've seen up close: a parent who struggles with a smartphone, a creator juggling too many channels, a fan who wants everything in one place.

How we build our apps

  • Keep it simple. Each app focuses on the things people actually need it for.
  • Be clear about your data. Each app has its own privacy policy that spells out exactly what it stores and where.
  • Be clear about pricing. Each app shows what's free and what costs money before you pay.
  • Real support. When you email us, one of the two of us reads it and writes back.

Our apps

  • Upload Manager: video planner for creators
  • DigiAid: senior digital helper for iPhone
  • CheckItOff: checklists, flashcards and Focus Lock
  • BudgetGenie: budget, bills and receipts
  • MOBIT: habit and money tracker
  • Bias Pocket: K-pop fan companion
