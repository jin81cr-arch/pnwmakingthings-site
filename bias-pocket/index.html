Bias Pocket privacy policy

Made by PNW Making Things.
Effective October 31, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

Bias Pocket is a fan-made K-pop app made by PNW Making Things ("we"). We built it so most of what you do stays on your own phone. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what goes online, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Your Bias Pocket picks, calendar events, concert trips, photocard binder, notes, fun facts, links, photos you add to profiles, lesson progress and settings are saved only on your device. We don't receive or store them. If you delete the app or use Settings → Delete my data, they are gone. Backups you create are files you control.

No accounts

Bias Pocket has no accounts and no sign-in. Nothing you add in the app is sent to us. The Fandom tab only opens Instagram or Threads; what you do there is covered by those apps' own policies.

Deals

The Deals page shows sponsored offers from brands. They are ads, marked "Sponsored." Viewing deals doesn't require an account, and we don't track which deals you view or tap. Purchases happen on the brand's own website under its own privacy policy.

News, videos and app updates

To show K-pop headlines and new videos, the app downloads public feeds from K-pop news sites (Soompi and NME) and YouTube. Like any website you visit, those services can see your device's IP address. We don't send them anything you add in the app.

Videos come from YouTube. Watching them is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

To deliver app updates, the app checks Expo's update service. It sends the app version and a random install ID that Expo uses to send the right update. The ID isn't linked to your name, email or advertising ID, and we don't use it to track you.

Phone calendar and photos

If you allow it, Bias Pocket reads and adds events to your phone's calendar and lets you pick photos from your library. This happens only on your phone. Nothing from your calendar or photos is uploaded.

Subscriptions

Pro is sold through Apple (and Google Play on Android). Apple handles your payment. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for Bias Pocket. We use this to unlock Pro and to see overall subscription numbers. It isn't linked to your name or email.

  • Pro costs $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year as an auto-renewing subscription, or $39.99 once for lifetime access. The price is also shown in the app before you buy.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. You aren't charged if you cancel before the trial ends.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
  • Use of the app is covered by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase hosts the list of deals (servers in the United States). It doesn't receive anything you add in the app. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat checks Pro subscriptions and gives us anonymous subscription statistics. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Expo delivers app updates. See the Expo privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google handle payments under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No ad tracking. The only ads are the sponsored offers on the Deals page.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Delete everything in Settings → Delete my data, or by deleting the app.
  • Cancel Pro anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

Bias Pocket isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The services listed above receive only the technical information described in this policy, such as an install ID or IP address.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, let you know in the app.
