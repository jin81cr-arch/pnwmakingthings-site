Bias Pocket privacy policy and terms

Made by PNW Making Things LLC.
Effective October 31, 2026. Updated November 20, 2026.

Bias Pocket is a fan-made K-pop app made by PNW Making Things LLC ("we"). We built it so most of what you do stays on your own phone. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what goes online, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Your Bias Pocket picks, calendar events, concert trips, photocard binder, notes, fun facts, links, photos you add to profiles, your own profile picture, your lightstick message, lesson progress and settings are saved only on your device. We don't receive or store them. If you delete the app or use Settings → Delete my data, they are gone. Backups you create are files you control.

No accounts

Bias Pocket has no accounts and no sign-in. Nothing you add in the app is sent to us. The Fandom tab only opens Instagram or Threads; what you do there is covered by those apps' own policies.

Deals

The Deals page shows sponsored offers from brands. They are ads, marked "Sponsored." Viewing deals doesn't require an account, and we don't track which deals you view or tap. Purchases happen on the brand's own website under its own privacy policy.

News, videos and app updates

To show K-pop headlines and new videos, the app downloads public feeds from K-pop news sites (Soompi and NME) and YouTube, including any YouTube channels you choose to follow in the app. Like any website you visit, those services can see your device's IP address. We don't send them anything you add in the app.

Videos come from YouTube. Watching them is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

To deliver app updates, the app checks Expo's update service. It sends the app version and a random install ID that Expo uses to send the right update. The ID isn't linked to your name, email or advertising ID, and we don't use it to track you.

Phone calendar, camera and photos

If you allow it, Bias Pocket reads and adds events to your phone's calendar, lets you pick photos from your library, and lets you take a photo with the camera. This happens only on your phone. Nothing from your calendar, camera or photos is uploaded.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders and allow notifications, Bias Pocket shows a notification for the birthdays and debut anniversaries of your picks and for events you add. These reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. No reminder information is sent to us or to any server, and we don't send marketing notifications.

Subscriptions

Pro is sold through Apple (and Google Play on Android). Apple handles your payment. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for Bias Pocket. We use this to unlock Pro and to see overall subscription numbers. It isn't linked to your name or email.

  • Pro costs $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year as an auto-renewing subscription. The price is also shown in the app before you buy.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. You aren't charged if you cancel before the trial ends.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
  • Use of the app is covered by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase hosts the list of deals (servers in the United States). It doesn't receive anything you add in the app. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat checks Pro subscriptions and gives us anonymous subscription statistics. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Expo delivers app updates. See the Expo privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google handle payments under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No ad tracking. The only ads are the sponsored offers on the Deals page.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Delete everything in Settings → Delete my data, or by deleting the app.
  • Cancel Pro anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

Bias Pocket isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The services listed above receive only the technical information described in this policy, such as an install ID or IP address.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, let you know in the app.

Terms of Use

Fan-made app

Bias Pocket is an independent fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any artist, group, agency or label. Group and member information comes from public sources and may be incomplete or out of date. If you spot a mistake, use the "Report a mistake" link in the app or email us.

Copyright and your photos

Bias Pocket doesn't include, sell or recommend copyrighted photos, music, videos, album art or logos. The app uses its own original artwork.

You can add photos from your own library or camera to personalize the app. Only add photos you took yourself or have the right to use. Photos you add stay on your phone for your personal, private use. Bias Pocket doesn't upload, publish or share them, and there is no way to post them to other people in the app. You are responsible for the photos and text you add, including your lightstick message and name card.

Names, news and videos

Artist, group, agency, fandom and brand names belong to their owners. They are used only to identify them and don't mean the owners endorse Bias Pocket. News headlines come from the publishers' public feeds and link to the full article on the publisher's own site. Videos and social media open in YouTube, Instagram or Threads. Bias Pocket doesn't copy or host that content.

Copyright concerns

If you believe something in Bias Pocket uses your copyrighted work or trademark without permission, email support@pnwmakingthings.com. Include your contact details, a description of the work, and where it appears in the app. We'll review it and remove or change anything that shouldn't be there.

Deals

Deals are paid ads from brands, marked "Sponsored." The brand is responsible for its offer, products and website. Any purchase is between you and the brand.

Bias Pocket Pro

Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, offered monthly or yearly. The price, trial and renewal terms are listed under Subscriptions on this page and shown in the app before you buy. Use Restore Purchases in the app to unlock Pro on another device with the same Apple Account.

Lightstick mode

Lightstick mode makes your screen bright and can flash or pulse. Please don't use the moving effects if flashing lights affect you, and follow the venue's rules about phones and lights at concerts.

The app can open other websites and apps, such as ticket sellers, brand shops, YouTube, Instagram and Threads. We aren't responsible for those sites. Anything you buy there is between you and that seller.
